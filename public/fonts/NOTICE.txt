Boldonese Cyrillic (display, locale ru)
Version: v9.1
Family CSS: Boldonese Cyrillic
File: BoldoneseCyrillic-Regular.woff2

Covers large Cyrillic brand/display headings, Latin notes, punctuation and
pedagogical combining acute accents from the same face. Compact headings use
Unbounded and body/UI text uses Inter. The "ESPAÑOL HONESTO" wordmark stays
in the original Latin Boldonse. U+2116 NUMERO SIGN is intentionally supplied
by the complete Unbounded fallback because it is not present in this binary.

This face embeds material derived under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:
- Boldonse — see licenses/Boldonse-OFL.txt
- Onest — see licenses/Onest-OFL.txt

Name "Boldonese Cyrillic" is used without a Reserved Font Name restriction from Boldonse.
The font may be embedded in Español Honesto; it must not be sold as a standalone font product.
